## Caption

A quarter of Canadian businesses now plan to use AI in the next year. Two years ago it was about 1 in 10.

That's a big jump in a short time. Most of these businesses aren't planning anything flashy, just help with everyday work like follow-ups, scheduling and reports.

If you're one of the other three quarters, now is a good time to look at where it could fit.

Source: Statistics Canada, Canadian Survey on Business Conditions, Q3 2026
